What's new in RečPoReč 12.0?

1. The app now remembers which shelf (tab) you were on: All, New, Started, or Finished.
2. Before Play/Pause, there's a new button: Rewind sentences. Type in how many sentences you want to go back, and the app remembers that number. Long-pressing repeats the last rewind. For example, if you typed five sentences, it keeps that number until you change it.
3. The screen reader now also announces the exact number of sentences the Rewind sentences button will go back, not just its name.
