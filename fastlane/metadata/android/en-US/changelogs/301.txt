What's new in RečPoReč 11.0?

1. Now, when you're on an article on your phone and tap Share, RečPoReč is offered as one of the options. The app reads the text aloud without permanently storing it.
2. Long-pressing the "Export settings to file" button creates a backup of all documents in the app.
3. Long-pressing the "Import settings from file" button restores that backup from where you saved it.
