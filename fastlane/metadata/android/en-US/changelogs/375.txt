What's new in RečPoReč 12.0?

1. The app now remembers which shelf (tab) you were on: All, New, Started, or Finished.
2. Settings now includes navigation by sentences - choose 1, 3, 5, or 10 sentences per step.
3. General voice settings (language, voice, speed, volume, pitch, combined voices) now apply to every document, instead of being set separately per document.
4. Removed the separate per-document buttons for pitch, speed, volume, language, voice, and combined voices from the reader itself - all of that is now set in one place.
5. "Auto-scroll document" and "Schedule reading" are now their own regular buttons in the reader, no longer hidden behind a long-press.
6. The Options button was added to the reader too, top-right - same as the main screen, so you don't have to leave the book to change settings.
7. Long-pressing Options now checks permissions, and long-pressing Add document now announces statistics.
8. "Reset to defaults" now resets absolutely everything at once, on both settings screens.
9. Buttons are now aligned and evened out, with larger text for better readability.
10. Fixed tooltip texts that were giving stale, incorrect information.
11. Navigation by minutes consolidated into one option with a minute-count picker, instead of three separate entries.
12. Fixed combined voices not resetting to defaults.
13. For the Shake and Auto-read switches - turning them on now immediately opens the picker (shake intensity / when to read), instead of a list that stays permanently visible underneath. Long-pressing the switch reopens the picker to change it later.
14. Those same switches now show the currently selected option in their own text (e.g. "Shake phone... - Light"), so you don't have to remember what you picked.
15. Shortened all text-entry dialogs (search, go to page/minute, bookmarks, wake-up, scheduled reading, rename, combined voices) - just the input field and Cancel, the keyboard itself confirms.
