What's new in RečPoReč 11.0?

1. Now, when you're on a document or book on your phone and tap Share, RečPoReč is offered as one of the options.
2. Also, in a document's own options, there's now a Share option too, so you can share it with another app. Handy when you want to send someone a book you liked straight from the reader.
3. Also added to the actions for a single document: Export to txt. It includes basic cleanup.
4. If you accidentally delete one or all documents, you now have a window to change your mind and undo it.
5. A "Create backup" button was added to the main screen - it backs up all your books, including where you left off.
6. Long-pressing that same button restores from a backup. The backup file is deleted afterward, so nothing piles up.
7. Reorganized book list on the main screen - there's now also a "New" tab.
8. In the reader's top row, the fourth button now selects all text, and long-pressing it copies the selection. No cutting, pasting, or editing - just the basics. This isn't an editor, it's a reader.
9. Added reading of image formats too. That text is automatically exported to txt - simpler this way. The same applies to image-based (scanned) PDFs. Not 100% reliable, but useful. Not sure about Cyrillic yet - Latin script works solidly, tested.
10. The app now remembers which shelf (tab) you were on: All, New, Started, or Finished.
