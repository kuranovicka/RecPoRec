What's new in RečPoReč 10.0?

1. The app now shows up properly in the notification shade, and playback starts right away when you open it from there.
2. The app no longer reads out those strange stray symbols ("hieroglyphs") that sometimes appeared in documents.
3. A Back button is now shown next to Play/Pause in every situation, even when all other controls are hidden.
4. Long-pressing the app icon on the home screen (the same gesture used for shortcuts) now offers the option to continue or pause reading. This means we no longer need to open the last document directly.
5. Long-pressing the Add document button now checks whether we've missed granting any permission - useful if something was accidentally skipped.
6. Long-pressing Options on the main screen now reads out reading statistics across all documents: total listened time, how much is left, which document you've spent the most time on, and how many documents you have in total.
7. Long-pressing the Exit button checks the connection status of Bluetooth devices, and offers to connect manually, in case automatic connection didn't work.
8. Settings export/import was added - useful when switching phones and reinstalling with the same voices as before.
9. Physical media button support on Bluetooth devices has finally been fixed, as well as pause/resume via tap on classic Bluetooth headphones.
10. Most importantly, two-finger tap on screen finally pauses and resumes reading too.
11. Since two-finger pause/resume is now available, there's no longer a need for yet another way to pause - shaking the phone. So now, if enabled in settings, shaking the phone extends the timer instead, just like in a smart audiobook player.
12. In place of extending the timer, that same button (long-press) now offers auto-scrolling through the document. Choose by pages, bookmarks, minutes, or chapters (if any). Choose forward or backward. Stop scrolling with the play/pause button.
13. Small fixes around button sound feedback, the timer, and document auto-scrolling.
